What we do

One partner from design to operation

Procurement

Thanks to our extensive supplier network, we find the most favourable purchasing and shipping solutions for our clients: solar panels, mounting structures and inverters.

Construction

Coordination and full turnkey construction of solar investments. In recent years we have successfully built more than 20 MW of PV plants.

Operation

Real-time production monitoring, maintenance, scheduling and emergency response, so that your plant keeps producing reliably.

Routine and preventive services

These include the maintenance and upkeep of electrical and mechanical equipment, and replacement of parts in case of warranty faults.

  • Real-time production monitoring and analysis
  • Grounds maintenance
  • Production-related reporting and scheduling in cooperation with Ewiser Ltd.
  • Alarm handling and emergency response
  • Regular inspection of protection equipment (surge arresters, combiner boxes, circuit breakers)
  • Condition checks of the solar panels (cleaning in case of extreme soiling)
  • Checking inverter operation and fault messages
  • Cleaning of cables, fans, vents and connections
  • Inspection of connections

About us

Czech roots, Hungarian projects

Eogen Ltd. was founded in 2010 as the Hungarian subsidiary of the Czech company Eogen A.S. Besides operating 15 MW of solar parks it built itself, Eogen A.S. currently also monitors, maintains and operates a further 30 MW of solar parks for its clients in the Czech Republic.

Building on that industry experience, our Hungarian operations focus on two main areas: developing solar parks and trading in solar system components. Since July 2016, the trading activity has been carried out by Eogen Hungary Ltd.

The Mezőhegyes pilot project and our current activity

Mezőhegyes 500 kW solar power plant. In early 2016 Eogen Ltd. completed its pilot investment, building a small solar power plant of under 500 kW in Mezőhegyes. During construction we applied our earlier experience and the latest technological advances, and as a result we were able to deliver the project at outstanding quality. Since July 2016 the plant's output has been sold under a KÁT (mandatory feed-in) balancing group agreement. Its production data is public and can be viewed on sunnyportal.com (under the name Mezőhegyes 1).

Current activity. Until the end of 2019 the Eogen group developed a 10+10 MW solar project pipeline in two phases. Besides our own projects, we also undertake the coordination and full construction of solar investments for our clients, as well as the supply of solar panels, mounting structures and inverters. Under such investments we have built a further 4 MW in total at Kistelek, Szentes and Balástya.
